<p>I am <strong>Junteng Liu</strong>, a first-year PhD candidate at the <strong>HKUST NLP Group</strong>, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, supervised by <strong>Professor Junxian He</strong>. I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.</p>

  <li>LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability</li>
</ul>
